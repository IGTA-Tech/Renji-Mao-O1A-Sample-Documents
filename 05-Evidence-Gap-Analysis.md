| Gap | Priority | Action Required |
|-----|----------|-----------------|
| GPI methodology explanation | MEDIUM | Obtain from GPI or expert |
| Official GPI ranking certificate | MEDIUM | Request from GPI |
| Association vs. ranking distinction | LOW | Address in brief |

### Challenges

Professional poker lacks traditional membership associations like medical boards or bar associations. The GPI ranking system serves an analogous function but may require explanation for adjudicators unfamiliar with poker.

### Strengthening Recommendations

1. **Obtain official GPI ranking documentation** - Request official ranking certificate or letter
2. **Include GPI methodology explanation** - Document how rankings are calculated
3. **Add expert letter** - Explain GPI's role as de facto membership organization
4. **Consider alternative framing** - May be stronger to present as supporting rather than primary criterion

### Assessment

This criterion is satisfied but is the weakest of the claimed criteria. Recommend using as secondary support rather than leading with it.

### Mitigation Strategies

**1. Poker as Athletics/O-1A Category**

- Impact: LOW - Established precedent exists

**2. Association Membership**
- Risk: GPI rankings challenged as not "membership"
- Mitigation: Frame as secondary criterion, include expert explanation
- Impact: MODERATE - May need to argue alternative interpretation

**3. Expert Letters**
- Risk: Insufficient expert validation
- Mitigation: Obtain 3-5 quality letters before filing
- Impact: MODERATE - Letters significantly strengthen case
