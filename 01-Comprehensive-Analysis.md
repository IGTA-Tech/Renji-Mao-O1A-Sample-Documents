# REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

## Statutory Basis

The O-1A nonimmigrant classification is established under **Section 101(a)(15)(O)(i) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA)**, which provides for the admission of:

> "An alien who has extraordinary ability in the sciences, arts, education, business, or athletics which has been demonstrated by sustained national or international acclaim and whose achievements have been recognized in the field through extensive documentation..."

## Regulatory Criteria Under 8 CFR § 214.2(o)(3)(iii)

A petition for an alien of extraordinary ability must include evidence demonstrating sustained national or international acclaim. The regulation provides that such evidence **shall include at least three (3) of the following**:

| Criterion | Description | Applicability to Mr. Mao |
|-----------|-------------|--------------------------|
| **(A)** | Nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards | **EXCEPTIONAL** - Two WSOP Bracelets |
| **(B)** | Membership in associations requiring outstanding achievements | **MODERATE** - GPI Rankings, WSOP Circuit |
| **(C)** | Published material about the beneficiary | **STRONG** - PokerNews, VIP Grinders, CardPlayer |
| **(D)** | Judging the work of others | **LIMITED** - Potential expert commentary |
| **(E)** | Original contributions of major significance | **STRONG** - Strategic innovations, comeback techniques |
| **(F)** | Authorship of scholarly articles | **LIMITED** - Potential strategy content |
| **(G)** | Critical or essential capacity for distinguished organizations | **STRONG** - WSOP bracelet winner status |
| **(H)** | High salary or remuneration | **STRONG** - $4.2M+ career earnings |
